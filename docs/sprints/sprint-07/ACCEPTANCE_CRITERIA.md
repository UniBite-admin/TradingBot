# Sprint 07 - Acceptance Criteria

Sprint 07 is acceptable only when the project has a credible, explicit validation story and the risk/accounting model remains conservative.

## Completion conditions

1. Cost assumptions are separated into fee, spread, slippage, liquidity, and latency categories.
2. The risk engine can reject threats without reliance on strategy output.
3. The ledger can reconstruct trade outcomes from domain events and recorded fees.
4. Validation reports are explicit about which assumptions are realistic and which are still provisional.
5. The stage gate blocks any progression until the evidence is reviewed.

## Required evidence

- cost-model documentation,
- risk decision logs,
- ledger integrity checks,
- validation reports labeled as research or stress-test results.

## Non-criteria

- no profitability assertion,
- no live approval,
- no scaling approval,
- no final strategy approval.
